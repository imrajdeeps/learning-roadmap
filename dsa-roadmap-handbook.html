How to use this handbook
  1. Read the docs tab — understand the idea and its complexity before writing any code.
  2. Implement it once from scratch, without copying. This is the step people skip.
  3. Work the interview Q&A — try each question on paper first, then compare with the solution.
  4. Grind the practice problems in the listed order, easy to hard.
  5. Write your notes in your own words while it's fresh. Re-solve anything you struggled with after 3–4 days.
